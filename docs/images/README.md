## The engravings

Every plate is an engraving from a public collection, recoloured in the two inks of its theme. The full list with titles, engravers, dates and object numbers is in StrixUI's `design-system/assets/Wallpapers/README.md`.

| Plate | Engraving | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Saturnus (banner, desktop) | Hendrick Goltzius after Polidoro da Caravaggio, Saturnus, from Eight Pagan Gods, 1592. Art Institute of Chicago 2023.968 | CC0 |
| Janus (lock screen) | Robert van Audenaerd after Carlo Maratta, Janus opens the door of the year, 1685 to 1723. Rijksmuseum RP-P-OB-67.432 | Public domain |
| The other gods (`gods.jpg`, theme picker, the other screens) | Engravings from the Art Institute of Chicago and the Rijksmuseum, listed in StrixUI | CC0 or public domain |
